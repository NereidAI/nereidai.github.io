# About NereidAI

<img class="nr-about-logo" src="/assets/logo.png" alt="NereidAI logo" width="120" />

## The name

**"Nereid" is simply the mascot name we chose — it carries no hidden or
particularly deep meaning.** In Greek mythology, the Nereids are the *spirits of
the sea* (海之精灵), daughters of Nereus. We liked the image, so we made it the
project's mascot and the shared prefix for every product.

## What the mascot stands for

Picture a spirit swimming through an ocean of data:

- **navigating the sea of data** — finding a path through the devices, models, and
  pipelines of an AIoT system;
- **helping users find their way** — turning a fragmented edge-to-cloud stack into
  one clear, closed loop;
- **spotting the hidden reefs** — vendor lock-in, mandatory cloud dependence, data
  leaving the customer perimeter, and models that never make it to the field.

The reefs are exactly what the platform is designed to steer around.

## Our mission

> **Democratize AIoT** by integrating edge and cloud workflows into one seamless
> closed loop — low-latency edge inference, privacy-preserving operations, and
> automated model monitoring, all in a single platform.

In practice that means a small team can keep turning its own business data into
models that run on its own field devices: data never has to leave the customer's
perimeter, there is no mandatory cloud, and no single silicon or edge vendor is
baked into the platform.

## How the family is named

Every product shares the **Nereid** prefix; the suffix says what it does:

| Name | The suffix suggests |
|---|---|
| NereidSphere | the sphere that holds the suite together — portal, gateway, glue |
| NereidIdentity | identity and access |
| NereidLink | linking devices into the platform |
| NereidForge | the forge where models are made |
| NereidHorizon | reaching the distant horizon of remote edge nodes |
| NereidEdge | the edge, on field gateways |
| NereidFleet | a fleet of robots |
| NereidMobile | the mobile window |

The commercial industry solutions follow the same spirit — NereidFactory,
NereidSentinel, NereidMedical. See [Naming & abbreviations](../reference/naming.md).

## What we believe

- **Private by design** — fully air-gapped operation, no mandatory call-home.
- **Open source, commercially supported** — Apache-2.0 community edition; enterprise
  LTS and turnkey delivery under commercial terms.
- **Loosely coupled products** — every product stands alone; the suite layer only
  federates them. See [Standalone by default](../products/index.md#standalone-by-default).
- **Vendor-neutral contracts** — no hard dependency on one edge stack or one silicon
  vendor.
