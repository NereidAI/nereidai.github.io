# Support

Everything below routes through the open project or through a commercial
agreement. There is no support email address and no ticket portal published on
this site — the [NereidAI organization on GitHub](https://github.com/NereidAI) is
the single entry point.

## Getting help from the community

Community support flows through GitHub. There is **no free technical support** and
**no SLA** — but the community and the maintainers are active in the open.

| If you want to… | Go to… |
|---|---|
| Report a bug or a reproducible problem | GitHub **issues** in the relevant repository |
| Ask a question, share an idea, show what you built | GitHub **Discussions** |

!!! tip "Before you post"
    Search existing issues and Discussions first. When you open a new one, include
    the version (`v0.0.1`), your environment, and clear steps to reproduce.

## What commercial support adds

| | Community | Commercial |
|---|---|---|
| Channel | GitHub issues / Discussions | Named engineer |
| Response | Best effort, no SLA | Contractual response commitment |
| Fixes | Latest release | Backported to LTS versions |
| Deployment | Self-service | Turnkey air-gapped delivery and on-premises private POC |
| Knowledge transfer | Docs and community | Private training |

Commercial support is defined and delivered under a **separate commercial
agreement**. See [License & Support](license.md) and
[Community vs Enterprise](community-vs-enterprise.md).

## Contact

For enterprise inquiries — commercial support, deployment, customization, NDA
references, or collaboration — please reach out through the
**[NereidAI organization on GitHub](https://github.com/NereidAI)** (organization
owners / Discussions).

No other contact channel is published on this site.

??? note "What community support is not"
    Community support does not include contractual response times, private
    engineering time, deployment on your premises, or backported fixes to older
    releases. Those are commercial capabilities — see
    [Community vs Enterprise](community-vs-enterprise.md).
